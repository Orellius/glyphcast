glyphcast brand

The mark is a play triangle rasterized into quadrant glyph cells — generated by the same cell grammar the codec uses, not drawn. It exists as vectors and as four lines of plain Unicode.

usage

  • Prefer the mark on dark. On light, use the light-bg variants — don't dim the standard green.
  • The wordmark is always lowercase monospace: glyphcast.
  • Don't redraw, skew, outline, or add effects to the mark. It is generated — regenerate it (bun scripts/gen-brand.ts) if it ever needs to change.
  • In any plain-text context, use the text logo. That's the point.
